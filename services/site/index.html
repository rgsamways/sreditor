What it is

Sreditor is a CLI that judges your team's work against the CRA's actual three-part SR&ED test — technological uncertainty, systematic investigation, technological advancement — in near real-time, as changes are archived, not once a year from memory. Every judgment prompt is public, plain TypeScript, and deliberately skeptical rather than generous, because a defensible claim is worth more than a generous one.

How it works

  1. sreditor probe
    Before you build, articulate the genuine uncertainty in your own words — or find honestly that there isn't any.
  2. sreditor judge
    After a change is archived, it's judged against the real CRA test — not "was this hard," but "was this technologically uncertain."
  3. sreditor rollup
    Related changes get grouped into one coherent, CRA-shaped project narrative — the level CRA actually wants a claim framed at.
  4. sreditor report
    A T661 Part 2-structured document, with real word counts against the real per-line limits.

Those last two numbers aren't in tension — they're answering different questions. Most individual changes are, correctly, too routine to be eligible on their own; a real SR&ED narrative usually only becomes visible once several related changes are read together as one investigation.
